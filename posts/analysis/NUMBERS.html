Ferrari's tarn, in numbers

← The instruments2 of 2Contents →

A reference card for anyone painting in this language. Everything below was measured on Mark Ferrari's Alpine Tarn at Dusk at its native 634×469 pixels, with the scripts in toolkit/. How each was measured, and how to read it, is in the tutorial. All values are CIE L* (0 black, 100 white). "Squint" means blurred with a Gaussian of σ = 2.5 native pixels.

The file

  • 1268×938 PNG, an exact 2× nearest-neighbour upscale: 634×469 native, 172 colours.
  • Pure black (0,0,0) is 19% of all pixels.

  • Three-value notan: groups centred at 16 / 42 / 70; cuts at 28.9 and 55.6.
  • Balance: 51% dark, 27% middle, 21% light.

  • Nothing is white. The sky runs L* 71–79, and only a few crest pixels pass 80.

Light

  • Low sun from the left, slightly behind the scene. Lit faces are warm (b* > 12); shade is violet or dull olive.
  • The sky is iso-luminant: flat across the columns (L* 73.5–74.7); from top to horizon, violet (L* 71.5, b* −8.7) to peach (L* 79, b* +21).
  • Ground brightens as it recedes: left bank L* 30–45 at the bottom to 53 at its far edge; right bank 5 to 20.
  • Hummocks and the island: brightest just under the crest (island L* 52), dark at the base (27).

Reflection

  • An exact mirror. The axis is the far shoreline (row 228), with vertical scale 1.00.
  • Reflected minus source: lights (L* > 55) −13.1, mids (30–55) −4.5, darks (< 30) +1.2; a* −5.1.
  • Reflected sky is about 4 L* darker than the sky.

Edges (rise width, 20→80%)

  • Silhouettes against sky: 50% hard (≤ 2 px), median contrast 69 L*.
  • Banks and grass interiors: 58–64% soft (> 4.5 px).
  • Lost edges, as ΔL* across the boundary: far banks against their reflections 0.3; framing trees against far forest 2.5; mountain against sky 5.

Palette

  • Two hue families: sun (ochre and orange, b* +15 to +45) and air (violet-pink, b* −15 to +20). The only greens are dark olives (a* < 0).
  • Chroma peaks at mid values (L* 40–70) and collapses in the darks.
